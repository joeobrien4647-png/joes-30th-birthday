## 9. Functional requirements

**Hybrid interactivity** — static briefing + Firebase-synced item checklists + printable PDF fallback.

1. **Briefing block** (static) — timing, meeting point, payment, WhatsApp link, "drivers meet us at chateau" callout listing the 9 driving guests.
2. **Trolley cards** (one per trolley) — captain + helpers, category, budget, aisle hint, watch-out, item list rendered as live tickbox checklist.
3. **Live sync** (Firebase) — when any phone ticks an item, all phones update. Matches the existing pattern from leaderboard / messages / livefeed.
4. **Robin's dietary banner** — sticky callout on the page summarising the GF+DF + veggie cohort and what to look for.
5. **Printable PDF fallback** — downloadable from the page, one cover page + one page per trolley + dietary page + logistics page. Generated from the same data (single source of truth).
6. **Day 2 shop placeholder** — short note on the page: "Team-dinner shop is Thu morning, captains lead their own runs" with link to a future Day 2 plan.

---

## 10. Non-functional requirements

- **Mobile-first** — everyone's on phones in store.
- **Offline-tolerant** — checklists keep working with patchy signal, sync when online.
- **Printable** — PDF renders without JavaScript; readable at A4 portrait, big enough text for 50-something parents.
- **No login lock** — visible to all guests after the existing site login (consistent with rest of joes30.com).
