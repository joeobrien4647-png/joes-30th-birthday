Joe's Thirtieth

The time has finally come. After thirty years of growing up, getting into mischief, and dragging his closest friends along for every wild ride... Joe O'Brien is turning 30.

Twenty-six of his most beloved accomplices have answered the call. Together, they will descend on Château Roussignol in the Loire Valley, France, for six extraordinary days and five unforgettable nights.

Four teams will be forged. A leaderboard will track every triumph and every humiliation. Group games. Individual challenges. Cooking duties. Cleaning rotas. Everything counts. The full list of games will be revealed closer to the time.

A custom-built command centre at joes30.com holds the keys to everything: the full agenda, team assignments, cooking duties, FAQs, packing lists, expense tracking, and secrets yet to be revealed.

Every guest has their own personal login. Sign in. Study the plan. Prepare yourself.

There will be games. There will be chaos. There will be moments nobody will ever forget... and a few they will desperately try to.

Saturday night belongs to the 90s. Costumes are mandatory. Dignity is optional.

Some will return as legends. Others will return with regrets and a Ryanair hangover.

The adventure begins 29th April 2026.

May the birthday be with you.
