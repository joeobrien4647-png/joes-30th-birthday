Dear guest,

You made it onto the list. Here's what's waiting.

  • Wed 29 Apr Arrival Day ✈️

    Up at 2am, on French soil by breakfast. Check-in from 10:30am — straight to the château, no waiting around. Welcome BBQ in the evening. The trip starts properly.

  • Thu 30 Apr First Full Day ⛳

    Golf for the keen ones, yoga and croissants for everyone else. By lunch the group reunites and the team battles begin. Pool games, team dinner, evening games. Points are on the board.

  • Fri 1 May Adventure Day 🚦

    Canoe the Creuse river in the morning. Wine tasting on the terrace in the afternoon: a private sommelier, Loire Valley wines, teams competing blind. One of those days you'll be talking about for years.

  • Sat 2 May The Big Day 🎂

    Joe turns 30. Birthday Olympics on the lawn, pool party in the afternoon, birthday dinner at 7. Then the 90s Icons party: fancy dress mandatory, PopWorld in the garden, dancing till the neighbours complain.

  • Sun 3 May Last Full Day 🌳

    Lazy brunch to shake off the night before. Bellebouche: treetop ropes, mini golf, pedalos on the lake. One final evening together, outdoor cinema in the garden.

  • Mon 4 May Au Revoir ✈️

    Last croissants. Big clean. Group photo on the steps. Flight home from Poitiers at 16:15. The best week of the 20s.

There are games. There are prizes. There are forfeits.
And a few secrets that don't get revealed until you're actually there.

See you at Stansted.
Joe x
