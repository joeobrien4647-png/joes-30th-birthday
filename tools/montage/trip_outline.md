# Joe's 30th Birthday Trip — Video Montage Outline

Target runtime: **5 minutes (~300 seconds)**
Source: 626 photos and videos from a 6-day trip to **Château Roussignol, Loire Valley, France** (Wed 29 Apr – Mon 4 May 2026). 24 friends celebrating Joe O'Brien's 30th birthday.

Build the edit as an **emotional arc** across six chronological acts that match the actual days of the trip.

---

## Act 1 — Arrival (Day 1, Wed 29 Apr) ~25s, fast pace
2am leave for Stansted. Bleary-eyed airport selfies. 5:55am flight to Poitiers. First glimpse of the chateau at 10:30am. Shops run. First pool dip. Welcome BBQ at 8:30pm. Team Reveal late evening.
**Key beats:** airport, plane window, first chateau exterior, pool dive, BBQ flames, group cheers, late-night reveal moment.

## Act 2 — Settling In (Day 2, Thu 30 Apr) ~40s, building energy
Slow morning. Optional golf, yoga, pool. Long lazy afternoon. Team dinner outside. Evening games.
**Key beats:** morning yoga/golf, pool lounging, lunch spread, sunset, team dinner candles, board games.

## Act 3 — Adventure (Day 3, Fri 1 May) ~55s, peak action
Canoe team race on the Creuse — wet, chaotic, competitive. Lunch in the medieval village of Angles-sur-l'Anglin. Afternoon pool recovery. Team dinner + pre-birthday-eve toasts.
**Key beats:** canoes launching, paddling action, splashes, village exploration, post-race pool, dinner toasts.

## Act 4 — THE BIG DAY (Day 4, Sat 2 May) ~100s, peak energy + emotion ⭐
Joe's actual birthday. Birthday breakfast. **Birthday Olympics** — chaotic team games. Birthday BBQ + pool party. **90s ICONS costumes on at 5pm.** Birthday dinner. **Cake, candles, toasts, awards.** **90s ICONS PARTY** explodes at 10:15pm.
**Key beats:** birthday breakfast, Olympics chaos, BBQ + pool party, costume reveals, dinner spread, cake/candles close-up, toasts, party launch, dancefloor.
**This is the peak** — most cuts, biggest energy, longest section.

## Act 5 — Last Full Day (Day 5, Sun 3 May) ~45s, mellow + reflective
Lazy brunch (everyone hungover). Bellebouche outing: treetop accrobranche, mini golf, pédalos. Last group dinner. Outdoor cinema under the stars at 11pm.
**Key beats:** lazy brunch, treetop adventures, mini golf laughs, pédalo silliness, last dinner, cinema screen lit up under sky.

## Act 6 — Au Revoir (Day 6, Mon 4 May) ~35s, emotional, slow holds
Last breakfast. Packing up. Big chateau clean. **Final group photo at 10:30am.** Departures. Drive away. Flight home.
**Key beats:** last morning light on chateau, packing chaos, big clean, group photo (HOLD this shot 3+ seconds), hugs, cars driving away, plane window home.

## Editing rules

### Always include
- **Joe** (the birthday boy) in as many shots as possible
- Group shots > solo shots
- Laughter > posed
- Sunset / golden-hour landscapes between acts as breathing moments
- The chateau itself — establishing shots between days

### Always avoid
- Receipts, blurry photos, dark/indistinct shots
- Plain landscape with no people (except brief establishing shots)
- Duplicates of the same moment
- Anything embarrassing without context

### Pacing
- **Fast cuts** (0.4–0.8s per clip): Olympics, party, canoe race, costume reveals
- **Medium cuts** (1–2s): general trip life, meals, exploring
- **Slow holds** (2–4s): toasts, hugs, group photo, sunsets, emotional moments

### Title cards
- **Intro (3s):** "JOE 30 — LOIRE VALLEY 2026"
- **Day separators (1s each, minimal text):** "DAY 1" through "DAY 6" overlaid on the day's hero shot
- **Outro (5s):** "Au revoir 🥂 — see you for the 40th"

### Audio
- Music: epic / cinematic / building energy (e.g. "grand-adventure.mp3")
- Beat-sync the cuts during high-energy acts (3, 4)
- Original audio kept low under the music
- A clear audio moment of the toast / "happy birthday" sing-along in Act 4 — duck the music briefly
